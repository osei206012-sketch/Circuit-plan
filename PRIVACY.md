# Privacy Policy — Circuit Plan

_Last updated: [fill in date]_

Circuit Plan does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- All wiring plans you create are stored **only on your device**, using local on-device storage. They are never sent to a server.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- The app does not require an account, login, or network connection to function.
- The app requests no device permissions beyond what's needed to save your plan locally and, if you use it, let you save an exported image to your device.

If you delete the app, your saved plans are deleted with it (use the "Export PNG" button first if you want to keep a copy).
