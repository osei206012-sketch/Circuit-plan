Build your plan

Planning tool, not a substitute for a licensed electrician. Layouts and suggested gauges/breaker sizes are general U.S. residential rules of thumb. Have the finished plan and any real work reviewed and permitted by a licensed electrician before anything is energized.

Tap a device, then tap the grid to place it. Tap "Draw wire," then tap two devices to connect them. Tap a symbol or wire to select it; drag a symbol to reposition; double-tap to rename. The canvas scrolls if the plan is bigger than your screen.

"Devices" counts items wired to that circuit — not total electrical load. A licensed electrician should confirm actual VA load, run length and code before sizing anything for real.

Wire colors on this diagram distinguish circuits from each other for planning — they don't represent real insulation colors. On actual house wiring, black or red is typically hot, white is neutral, and bare copper or green is ground; always verify against your local code.
